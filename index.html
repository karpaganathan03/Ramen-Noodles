Broth Craftsmanship

The 18-Hour Simmer Alchemy

We never take shortcuts. No soup powders, no artificial concentrates. Just fire, spring water, quality ingredients, and relentless patience.

01

Water & Blanch

Filtered triple-purified water and marrow bones are blanched to purge impurities, creating a pristine canvas for extraction.

02

Rolling Boil

A fierce 18-hour rolling boil breaks down proteins and collagen, naturally emulsifying the liquid into rich liquid gold.

03

Aged Tare Drop

The soul seasoning: 100-year barrel-aged shoyu, toasted miso, or Okinawan salt combined with bonito and dashi reductions.

04

Aroma Oil & Sear

Finished with house-pressed black garlic or shallot aroma oil, torched melt-in-mouth chashu, and 45-second fresh noodles.
